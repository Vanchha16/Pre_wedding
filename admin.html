The Link Maker needs your Google Sheet. Open README.md and follow Step 2, then paste the Web app URL into assets/js/config.js → apiUrl.

To test on your computer first, run python tools/dev_server.py and open http://localhost:8000/admin.html.

Sign in

Use the password you set as ADMIN_KEY in the Google Apps Script.

1. Add guests

Type one guest per line. Khmer or English is fine. Each name gets its own link.

2. Message to send

{name} becomes the guest's name, {link} becomes their link.
